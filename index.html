My Approach

I don't just push pixels.
I solve business problems.

I operate at the intersection of product strategy and hands-on execution. Whether it's defining an MVP scope with the CEO, building a design system from scratch, or running A/B tests to validate assumptions. I own the outcome, not just the output.

01

Research-led, outcome-driven

Every design decision is backed by user research, competitive analysis, and business context. I don't design in a vacuum.

02

Systems over screens

I build scalable design systems and component libraries that reduce dev handoff friction and maintain consistency at scale.

03

Ship, measure, iterate

I integrate user feedback loops, A/B testing, and analytics into sprint cycles for evidence-based iteration.
